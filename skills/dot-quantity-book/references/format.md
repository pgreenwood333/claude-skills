# Approved binder format (from the engineer's approved binders)

## Title sheet + index (Word, Letter portrait)
- Margins: top/bottom 0.5" (720 twips), left/right 0.75" (1080). Body font: Word default (Times New Roman).
- Blue everywhere is #4472C4.
- Page 1, top block, each paragraph with a blue left border (single, 3 pt / sz 24, 8 pt space):
  - Firm name, bold, 11 pt, uppercase (e.g. ACME ENGINEERING). Spacing 10 pt before, 5 pt after.
  - Project name, 28 pt (e.g. "Main Street Bridge Replacement"). 5 pt before.
  - One line per project number, 13 pt: job/FMS numbers then federal-aid numbers, or the order the user gives
    (multi-project example: IM-0000-00(001), (003), (002)). First 7.5 pt before, last 7.5 pt after.
- "Quantity Calculations Binder", 12 pt blue, no border. 5 pt before, 10 pt after.
- The plan-set title sheet as an image, 6.5" wide (shrunk to fit the page when there are many numbers).
- "Final Plans", 13 pt blue, 15 pt before. No date.
- Page 2 (page break before): "Table of Contents" 16 pt blue, 10 pt after. One line per section: bold 11 pt black,
  title, right tab at 6.5" (9360 twips) with dot leader, page range ("3-4", "5", "6-10"). 6 pt after each line.

## Quantity calculations PDF
- Each calc sheet keeps its own orientation (most are landscape Letter; a portrait sheet stays portrait).
- Fit to one page wide; no content cut off at the edges; the engineer's typed corner page numbers removed.
- Page numbers bottom-center only, Helvetica 10 pt, about 0.25" above the bottom edge, starting at 3.
- Example binder: Asphalt, Tack Coat, Borrow, Sidewalk & Curb/Gutter 3-4; Vegetation Quantities 5; Earthwork
  Calculations 6-10 (Summary, Hand Calc, Phase 1-3); Structure Excavation (Pipes) 11; Permanent Erosion Control 12;
  Temporary Erosion Control 13.
